# Ship log

One public artifact per week. Small is fine. Late is not. This file is the evidence that you are
becoming the engineer, not just preparing to interview as one.

What counts: a blog post on shissorobotics.com or elsewhere, a published skill, a merged OSS PR,
a lab write-up with numbers, a talk, a design doc made public. What does not count: a draft.

Rule for every entry: **one claim, one piece of evidence.** "Hybrid retrieval beat dense by 9 points
of recall@10 on my 200-query set (CI 5–13)" is an entry. "Wrote about RAG" is not.
