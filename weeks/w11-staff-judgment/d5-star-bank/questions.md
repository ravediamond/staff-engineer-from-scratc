# Questions (answer with YOUR stories, 2 minutes each, recorded)

1. Tell me about the most impactful project you led end to end.
2. Tell me about a time you influenced a decision you did not own.
3. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or a senior leader.
4. Tell me about someone you grew into a stronger engineer.
5. Why staff at our company, and why now? (Paris unicorn version and Google/Meta version.)

## What a staff-level answer contains (no model stories: yours must be real)

1. Scope beyond your team (teams, users, money), the ambiguity you resolved, the technical bet and its alternatives, measured outcome, what you would change. Avoid "we" without saying what YOU did.
2. The stakeholders and their incentives, the data or prototype you used, how you made it easy to say yes (a doc, a pilot), the outcome, and what you did when part of it was rejected.
3. The disagreement on substance (not personality), how you raised it (privately, with data), the decision, disagree-and-commit or escalation done well, and the result, including if you were wrong.
4. A specific person, their starting point, what you deliberately did (stretch project, reviews, sponsorship), the evidence of growth (promotion, ownership), and how it scaled beyond them.
5. Paris unicorn: their stage and your fit (founding-staff energy, shipping, owning a whole surface, your eval/agent/RAG depth applied to their product), what you want to build there. Google/Meta: scope and scale you want, the team's specific problem, and your track record of moving big systems with measurement. Both: honest about leaving a long tenure ("8 years in one company: I've grown from data engineer to staff and want to apply that at a faster cadence") without negativity.
