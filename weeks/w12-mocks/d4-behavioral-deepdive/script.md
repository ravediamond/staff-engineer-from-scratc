# Interviewer script

## Behavioral (6 questions, ~6 min each, with the follow-ups)

1. Tell me about the project you are proudest of. *Follow-ups: what was your specific role? What would have happened without you? What did it change for the business, in numbers?*
2. Tell me about a time you had to align several teams with different priorities. *Follow-ups: who disagreed most and why? What did you give up?*
3. Tell me about a technical decision you made that turned out wrong. *Follow-ups: when did you realise? What did you do? What do you do differently now?*
4. Tell me about a time you raised the engineering bar beyond your own team. *Follow-ups: how did you get adoption? How did you measure it?*
5. Tell me about someone you mentored or sponsored. *Follow-ups: what specifically did you do? What did they achieve?*
6. Tell me about a time you pushed back on a leader or a deadline. *Follow-ups: what was the outcome? Would you do it again?*

## Project deep dive (45 min)

- Draw the architecture. Walk one request or one data item end to end.
- Which numbers prove it worked? How were they measured? What is the confidence interval?
- What were the two alternatives you rejected, and why?
- What broke in production? How did you find out? What changed after?
- Which part did you personally design or write? Which part did others own?
- If you rebuilt it today with twice the budget, what would you change? With half?
- (Edge-finding) Pick the deepest component and go three "why" levels down until the candidate says "I don't know". A staff answer says it clearly and says how they would find out.

## Rubric per answer (1–4)

| Axis | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | own tasks | team feature | cross-team problem | org-level outcome |
| Influence | none | persuaded peers | aligned teams with data | changed how the org decides |
| Execution | vague | delivered | delivered with measurement | delivered under ambiguity, measured, iterated |
| Conflict / judgment | avoids | escalates poorly | resolves on substance | resolves and improves the process |
| Self-awareness | blames | generic lessons | specific lessons | specific lessons + changed behaviour, with evidence |

Weights for a staff loop: scope 2, influence 2, execution 1, conflict 1, self-awareness 1. Floors: influence 3, scope 3.
