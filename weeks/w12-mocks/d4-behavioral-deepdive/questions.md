# Questions (after scoring)

1. Which answer was weakest, and which axis did it miss?
2. In the deep dive, where was the edge of your knowledge, and did you say so clearly?
3. Which story would you replace before a real loop, and with what?

## What good looks like

1. Name the axis, quote the moment ("I said 'we' for 3 minutes and never said what I did"), and the fix (rewrite that story's action section in first person with decisions).
2. "I don't know the internals of X; I'd find out by reading Y or measuring Z" is a strong answer. Bluffing past the edge is the most common deep-dive failure.
3. Replace task-shaped stories with scope-shaped ones; if none exist for an axis, that is input for what to seek at work next quarter (see `principal/`).
