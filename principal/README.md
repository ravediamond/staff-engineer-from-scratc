# Staff → Principal playbook

Getting the staff title is a hiring event. Becoming principal is a multi-year track record of being right about
important things, and making an organisation better at deciding. This folder is the long game: what changes,
how it is judged, and exercises you can start this quarter at your current job.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `01-what-changes.md` | scope, time horizon, outputs; the four archetypes; common failure modes |
| `02-how-it-is-judged.md` | what promotion committees and executives look for; the evidence packet |
| `03-operating-cadence.md` | how principals spend a week, a quarter, a year |
| `04-strategy-docs.md` | how to write a technical strategy (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions) |
| `05-exercises.md` | eight exercises grounded in your situation |

The honest frame: principal is rarely granted for excellence at staff work. It is granted when the org already
depends on your judgment beyond your scope. The exercises are designed to create that dependency on purpose.
