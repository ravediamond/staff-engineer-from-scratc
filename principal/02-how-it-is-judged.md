# 2. How principal is judged

Promotion committees and executives look for evidence across five areas. Build the packet continuously,
not the month before.

| Area | Question they ask | Evidence that convinces |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | What is different in the business because of you? | numbers at org level: revenue, cost, reliability, speed of delivery |
| Direction | Did you set direction others followed? | strategy docs adopted, roadmaps changed, decisions traced back to you |
| Judgment | Were your big calls right? | a record of bets with outcomes, including the ones you killed early |
| Leverage | How many people are more effective because of you? | platforms adopted, standards, engineers you grew into staff |
| Reputation | Who outside your team seeks your judgment? | requests from other orgs, external talks/OSS, executive trust |

## The evidence packet (keep it in your private repo)

- A **decision log**: date, decision, alternatives, expected outcome, actual outcome (review yearly).
- **Strategy documents** with their adoption trail.
- **Platform adoption** metrics over time.
- **People** you sponsored and where they are now.
- **External** artifacts and their reach.
