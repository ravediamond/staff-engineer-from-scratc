# 3. Operating cadence

## A week (suggested split)

| Share | Activity |
|---|---|
| 25% | Hands-on: prototypes, critical code reviews, incident deep dives (stay technically credible) |
| 25% | Design and strategy: writing, reviewing other teams' designs, decision meetings |
| 20% | People: mentoring staff/senior engineers, sponsoring, hiring loops |
| 15% | Cross-org alignment: product, research, infra, security, finance |
| 15% | Learning and external: papers, OSS, writing, talking to peers at other companies |

## A quarter

- One strategy or architecture document updated (or written), reviewed across orgs.
- Two to three critical designs reviewed in depth.
- One "paved road" improvement shipped (a platform capability, a template, a gate).
- A written retro of your bets: what changed, what you got wrong.

## A year

- A technical strategy refresh tied to company priorities.
- One big bet driven to a measurable outcome.
- Two people grown into larger scope.
- One external contribution with real reach.
