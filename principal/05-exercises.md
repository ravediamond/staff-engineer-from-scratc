# 5. Exercises (start now, at your current job)

These are real work, not homework. Each produces an artifact for your evidence packet.

1. **Diagnosis memo (2 pages).** Write the diagnosis section of an AI platform strategy for your company: what is
   actually broken, with numbers. Share it with your CTO as a draft for feedback, not a proposal.

2. **Decision log.** Start today. Log every significant technical decision you make or influence: alternatives,
   expected outcome. Review in 6 months; publish a (sanitised) lessons post.

3. **One paved road.** Pick one capability every AI team needs (tracing, eval gates, a harness template) and make it
   the default. Measure adoption monthly. This is your leverage evidence.

4. **Kill a project.** Find one initiative (yours or one you influence) that should stop. Write the case, with data.
   Principals are judged on what they stop as much as what they start.

5. **Sponsor someone.** Pick one engineer, agree on a stretch scope, meet every two weeks, and put their name on the
   outcome. Track their trajectory.

6. **Cross-org review.** Review one design outside your org per month. Write the review as you would want to receive it.

7. **External presence.** One talk or substantial post per quarter on evaluation or agent infrastructure, with real
   numbers. The ship log is the start; this is the next step.

8. **Strategy dry run.** Write the full strategy (diagnosis, policy, actions) for "AI engineering at <your company> in
   2028". Ask two senior people to attack it. Revise. Keep it in your packet whether or not it is adopted.
